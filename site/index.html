Power value is how many points a team's starting lineup is expected to score over the rest of the fantasy season. Each remaining week we start the best available QB, RBs, WRs, TE and FLEX by ESPN's projections, skipping byes and players ESPN expects to miss time, and replay the season 500 times with random injuries. So bench players count exactly as often as they'd really play: handcuffs earn credit when a starter goes down, stashed injured players earn it once they're back, and a backup QB who never sees the field adds almost nothing. Kickers and defenses are left out. "Starts X of Y wks" on each player is how often he makes that lineup. Bar segments are each position's share; the number is the team's rank there.
