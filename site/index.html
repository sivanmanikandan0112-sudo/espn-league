Power value measures how much better a roster is than what's sitting on waivers. Each player is worth his ESPN rest-of-season projection minus the best free agent's at his position. A team's best QB/RB/WR/TE/FLEX lineup counts in full and bench players count 30%; kickers and defenses are left out. That edge is added to what a lineup of free agents would score, then scaled so the top team shows 2,500. Each bar segment is that position's share of the edge, and its number is the team's rank at that position.
